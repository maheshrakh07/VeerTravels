About Prashant Tours & Travels

A Family Legacy Brand Based in Nashik Since 1988

Our Story

Prashant Tours & Travels is a family and legacy brand based in Nashik. Serving now for more than 35 years, we pride ourselves in our elevated service standards and unequaled reputation for courtesy and follow-through.

When you travel with us, you don't merely book your travel; we help you realize the dreams based on the idea of what kind of travel experience you seek.

Our journey began in 1988 with a simple vision: to make travel accessible, enjoyable, and memorable for everyone. Over the decades, we have grown from a small local agency to a trusted name in the travel industry, serving thousands of satisfied customers.

Proud Achievement

We are proud to be a preferred travel partner for The National Integration Tour for 10,000+ students conducted by special trains; meeting eminent personalities of the Parliament, namely Smt. Soniya Gandhi, Ex-President Smt. Pratibha Tai Patil, Shri. Sharad Pawar, Late Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, Ex-President Dr. Shankar Dayal Sharma, Ex-Prime Minister Shri. Chandra Shekhar
